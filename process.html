Methodology

The 7-Stage Architectural Journey

A transparent, strictly audited progression from the first concept sketch through bespoke quarry extraction to white-glove handover.

STAGE 01

Site Ecology & Solar Topography

Microclimate mapping, wind corridor analysis, and 3D LiDAR terrain scans.

STAGE 02

Volumetric Conception & Massing

Spatial sketches, light-well orientations, and preliminary structural physics.

STAGE 03

Material Curation & Sourcing

Quarry block selection in Italy, Portugal, and Rajasthan with client inspection.

STAGE 04

Permits, Approvals & Clearances

100% statutory clearance, environmental approvals, and civil permits.

STAGE 05

Monolithic Civil Execution

Subterranean waterproofing, post-tensioned slabs, and structural superstructure.

STAGE 06

Artisanal Joinery & Acoustic Envelope

Custom millwork, motorized glass facades, and architectural lighting integration.

STAGE 07

White-Glove Handover & Stewardship

Complete estate operation manual, 10-year structural warranty, and concierge maintenance.
