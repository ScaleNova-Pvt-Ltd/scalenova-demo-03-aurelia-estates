STAGE-BY-STAGE PROGRESSION

The Five Milestones of Acquisition

Click through the stages to review milestones, legal covenants, and architectural reviews.

Stage 01 • Discovery & Ecology

Site Ecology, Wind Corridors & Solar Topography

Before the first sketch is drawn, we deploy LiDAR drones to survey terrain elevation, map solar daylight arcs throughout the year, and chart seasonal wind paths to ensure natural passive cross-ventilation.

✓ 3D Solar Insolation Model Included
Stage 02 • Client Atelier Sessions

Personalized Volumetric Tailoring & Interior Layouts

You collaborate directly with our Principal Architect to adjust bedroom configurations, integrate private art collection display walls, and customize wine cellar and spa wellness rooms.

✓ Full Scale Physical 1:50 Architectural Model
Stage 03 • European Quarry Journey

Quarry Block Selection in Tivoli & Carrara

Clients are invited to personally inspect and tag monolithic travertine and marble blocks directly at Italian quarries before diamond-wire saw cutting and sea transit to India.

✓ Quarry Certificate of Origin & Authenticity
Stage 04 • Engineering Rigor

Monolithic Structural Pour & Sky-Frame Installation

M70 high-density self-compacting concrete poured under continuous ultrasound monitoring. Swiss Sky-Frame motorized glass walls installed by factory-trained Swiss glazing engineers.

✓ Zero-Water Ingress Guarantee
Stage 05 • The Key Handover

White-Glove Handover & Digital Estate Ledger

Complete title deed registration, RERA compliance documentation, hand-stitched leather-bound architectural monograph, and ScaleNova EliteOS smart home digital keys delivered.
