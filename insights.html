MONOGRAPH ABSTRACT

The Acoustic Sanctuary — Field Study

"The metropolitan decibel environment in cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore routinely peaks above 85 dBA. Conventional curtain wall construction admits low-frequency rumble, inducing sustained elevated cortisol. This paper explores our patented decoupling methodology that delivers NC-18 whisper silence."

Key Research Conclusions:
• Decoupled Cavity Walls: Staggered studs with mineral wool acoustic fill achieve STC 56 attenuation.
• Glass Interlayer: 1.52mm acoustic PVB targets 125 Hz road tire frequencies.
• HVAC Silencing: Gravity-fed slow displacement air eliminates turbulent register hiss completely.
