Lineage & Ethos

Architectural Dignity in a Disposable World

Founded in 2012 by master architect Julian Varma and structural engineer Sunita Rao, Aurelia was born out of resistance to the rapid, ephemeral glass boxes that came to dominate Indian skylines.

OUR CREED

Enduring Materiality

We believe a residence should gain character with the passage of decades, not decay. Our facades utilize hand-honed basalt, dry-stacked granite, and natural brass joinery that weathers into a deep, dignified patina.

TOTAL INTEGRATION

Architect + Builder in One Hand

Unlike conventional setups where design is outsourced to one firm and construction tendered to general contractors, Aurelia exercises complete in-house stewardship from first pencil stroke to key handover.
