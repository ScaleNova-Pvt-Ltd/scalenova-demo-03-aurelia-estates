Structural Integrity

Engineering the Indestructible

Beneath every surface of honed limestone lies uncompromising structural engineering. We design for centuries, not warranty cycles.

STRUCTURAL

Post-Tensioned Slabs

Eliminating intermediate support columns to create dramatic 18-meter uninterrupted living vistas and soaring ceiling envelopes.

GEOTECHNICAL

Deep Foundation Piling

Micro-piled directly into basalt bedrocks, isolated against ground vibration and certified under Zone II+ seismic resistance codes.

ACOUSTICS

NC-20 Sound Attenuation

Decoupled wall cavities, triple-glazed acoustic laminates, and silent gravity-fed HVAC plenums achieving library-level quietness.
